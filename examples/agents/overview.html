How it fits together

Scheduled runs can read your calendar and the subject lines of starred emails, but they can't change anything. Events only get added through a live chat with you.

How a run works

Every agent follows the same four steps. Your approval is always the last one.

1 · readGatherCalendar from Calendar.app, plus subject lines of emails you starred. All read-only.
2 · thinkSortFind what needs you, what's handled, and where the open time is.
3 · writeHand overA brief, a chat message, or a Notion page.
4 · youApproveReply "yes" to add blocks. Nothing changes until you do.

Good to know

  • Agents only read. They never create, move, or delete events and never send email while you're away.
  • The app needs to be open. If Claude is closed when a task is due, it runs the next time you open it.
  • Click "Run now" once per agent. Approving its tools then means scheduled runs won't stop to ask for permission.
  • Star an email to get a reminder. Agents see only the sender, subject, and date of starred emails. Unstar it when it's done.
  • Agents never open emails. No message bodies, links, or attachments, so codes and hidden instructions stay out of reach.
  • Approve read-only Gmail tools. On "Run now," allow search only. Deny send, forward, draft, and trash. That approval is the hard limit.
